Astra Times Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-26

Astra Times is a reflective astrology app. It uses your saved profile and selected context to generate timing guidance, chart analysis, planning views, and Ask responses.

Astra Times is intended for reflection and planning support. It does not provide guaranteed predictions or professional medical, legal, or financial advice.

Data you may provide

  • Name
  • Birth date
  • Birth time
  • Birthplace
  • Timezone
  • Current location or current-location context
  • Question text submitted in Ask
  • Chart and planning inputs derived from your saved profile
  • Selected profile photo
  • Saved Ask answers stored locally on your device
  • Notification preferences and local entitlement state

What stays on device

  • Saved profile details
  • Selected profile photo
  • Saved Ask answers and conversations
  • Theme and settings choices
  • Notification preference state
  • Local entitlement cache

What is sent to the hosted service

When you use Today, Ask, Plan, or Chart features, the app sends the profile fields and question or chart inputs needed to generate those results to the configured Astra Times backend over HTTPS.

That may include birth details, timezone, current-location context, question text you submit, and chart or timing inputs derived from your saved profile.

Optional permissions

  • Location, to help fill current-place context and timezone-aware guidance
  • Notifications, to send local timing reminders and alerts
  • Photos, to let you set a profile image

Core reading and chart flows should remain usable if you decline these permissions.

Tracking and advertising

Astra Times does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission for cross-app tracking.

Data use

Your data is used to personalize readings and chart views, provide Ask responses and planning guidance, restore the app to your saved local state, and schedule local reminders when you opt in.

Local controls and deletion

Using Log out in the app clears profile details, saved Ask state, local photo data, and local entitlement overrides on that device.

For support or privacy requests, contact taratimeapp@gmail.com.
